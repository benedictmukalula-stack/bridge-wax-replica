# Catalogue Cart Label Audit

The Water Pumps catalogue and its Domestic Pumps subsection now display `Add to cart` on every product card. Selected cards use `In cart`, and accessible labels use `Add [product] to cart` or `[product] is in the cart`. The header remains labeled `Cart`, while the underlying drawer still supports quotation requests to info@bridgewax.com.

The Water Pumps route was checked live and all 15 product cards showed the updated Cart action without remaining visible `Add to quote` or `In quote basket` labels.

The first product was selected live. Its card changed to `In cart`, the accessible label changed to `End-Suction Centrifugal Pump is in the cart`, and the header updated to `Cart 1`, confirming that the label change did not interrupt selection behavior.
