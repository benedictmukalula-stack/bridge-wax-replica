# Bridge Wax Replica — Ground-Truth Reference Specification

This is a reference-matched recreation request. The attached Bridge Wax source project is the authoritative visual and interaction specification; fidelity takes precedence over the default design guidance.

## Reference Direction

The site uses a restrained premium scientific/industrial aesthetic: a fixed white navigation bar, a deep ink-blue hero and footer, warm gold accents, editorial serif display typography, and practical sans-serif interface text. The visual language should feel established, precise, and quietly confident rather than playful or decorative.

## Design Movement

Contemporary scientific editorial design with understated luxury-brand cues.

## Core Principles

1. Preserve the reference's navy, cream, and gold hierarchy with strong contrast and generous whitespace.
2. Pair an editorial serif display face with a clean sans-serif body face; use typography to create credibility and calm.
3. Use image-led storytelling with restrained overlays, thin borders, and compact utility controls.
4. Keep interactions practical and tactile: clear active navigation, subtle hover states, mobile drawer navigation, and gentle scroll transitions.

## Color Philosophy

Deep ink navy establishes trust and technical seriousness. Warm gold is reserved for emphasis, contact actions, active states, and small signals of craft. Soft cream keeps the long-form sections warm and legible instead of clinical. White surfaces preserve clarity in the header and content areas.

## Layout Paradigm

The reference is a vertical editorial flow rather than a dashboard: fixed top navigation, a full-bleed hero slider, wide content bands, asymmetric image/text storytelling, and compact footer utilities. Sections should feel like framed chapters with clear visual pacing.

## Signature Elements

- A full-width hero carousel with numbered slide markers and a bottom-left content panel.
- Gold accent rules and small uppercase labels used as editorial wayfinding.
- Rounded, image-forward cards and dark navy information bands that transition between narrative sections.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should confirm user intent without competing with the content. Navigation changes state on scroll, slide controls remain obvious but quiet, cards lift only slightly on hover, and mobile navigation opens as a simple full-width panel.

## Animation

Use short opacity/transform transitions for hero copy, carousel changes, buttons, and mobile menu states. Avoid excessive spring motion or decorative looping effects. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep all UI transitions within approximately 300ms.

## Typography System

Use Playfair Display for hero statements, section headings, and editorial pull quotes. Use Inter for navigation, labels, supporting copy, and buttons. Display headings should be large, high-contrast, and line-broken intentionally; labels should use uppercase tracking with compact spacing.

## Brand Essence

Bridge Wax is a trusted scientific and laboratory solutions partner for teams that need dependable products, technical insight, and practical support. Personality: **precise, grounded, assured**.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are concise and confident. CTAs are direct and useful. Supporting copy is measured, specific, and avoids hype.

Example lines: “We exceed our vision.” and “Solutions that move science forward.”

## Wordmark & Logo

Use the supplied Bridge Wax mark from the reference assets in the header and favicon treatment rather than recreating it with a generic font.

## Signature Brand Color

Warm laboratory gold: `#c8963e`.
