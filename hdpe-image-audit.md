# HDPE Pipe Systems Image Audit

The current six HDPE catalogue paths returned HTTP 200 and distinct file hashes, but the cards were visually perceived as one repeated pipe image. The replacement set will use genuinely different HDPE subjects rather than crop-only variants.

The first two selected real-image candidates were inspected. The flange-plate image is a clean isolated product photograph with no people, visible watermark, or external branding. The fittings image is a clean isolated arrangement of black HDPE fittings on white with no people or visible watermark. Both are suitable for the catalogue.

The third candidate shows large fabricated HDPE storage or process tanks outdoors with no people, visible watermark, or external branding. The fourth candidate shows a clean isolated electrofusion coupling on white with no people, visible watermark, or external branding. Both are suitable for distinct HDPE catalogue cards.

The fifth candidate is a clean isolated HDPE flange adapter on white with no people, visible watermark, or external branding. The sixth candidate is a distinct black threaded HDPE fitting on white; it has no people or external logo/watermark. The final set therefore covers flange plates, assorted fittings, large fabricated pipe/tanks, electrofusion couplings, flange adapters, and threaded fittings.

The six final assets were normalized to 1400×1050 WebP, uploaded to managed storage, and wired to the six HDPE service scopes. All six return HTTP 200 and have unique content hashes. The full-page `/services/hdpe` verification shows six visibly distinct cards: flange plates, assorted fittings, fabricated tanks, an electrofusion coupling, a flange adapter, and a threaded fitting.
